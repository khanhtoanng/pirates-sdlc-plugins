# Intent — product outcome / feature only when needed

**Entry:** Extended Feature, or Feature outcome/actor/scope is unsettled or risk requires explicit recheck; not a mandatory Bug or Spike phase. Inspect the actual request and existing behavior; identify WHY, WHO, outcome, success signal, material scope boundary and non-goals. Resolve only significant product alternatives. **Exit:** short grounded intent and `READY | NEEDS_DECISION | BLOCKED`. Do not choose implementation or invent new features.
